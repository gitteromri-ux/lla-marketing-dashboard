1 What we changed this week · and why
AUDIENCE

From 11 interests to 41 interests AND a buyer filter

Every prospecting set now needs 2 signals: one of 41 interests (8 longevity/health, 14 education & self-development buyers, 19 supplements & training) AND Engaged Shoppers or top household income. Why: interest alone brought readers, the AND brings buyers.

COST

CPM from $352 to $67

We added Facebook Reels and Instagram feed next to the feed and widened ages to 35–65+ in the wide sets. Cheaper, larger pools of the same qualified people. Saturday averaged $25 CPM.

CREATIVE

4 UGC videos live, 2 sales on day one

Two presenters, square + vertical. Set 04 sold twice on Saturday night through Facebook Reels (Ad#17, Ad#18). UGC is now our cheapest traffic: 136 LP visits in 2 days.

STRUCTURE

6 ad sets, one clear job each

Banners only in sets 01/02. Sets 03–06 test video. Set 02 retargets 11 warm lists + 3 buyer lookalikes. Income set 05 targets top-10% households only. Advantage+ off, so Meta cannot drift outside the audience.
